About PrintingHub Dubai

A Bur Dubai print shop that treats your deadline like our own — since 2012.

Our Story

PrintingHub started in 2012 as a two-person shop in Al Shindagha with a single digital press and a simple promise: print it right, print it fast, and never make a customer chase you for an update.

Twelve years later, that promise hasn't changed — but almost everything else has grown. Today we run digital and offset presses, large-format printers, and a full finishing line (lamination, foiling, die-cutting, and binding) from our facility at the Blue Tin Elite Business Center, serving restaurants, clinics, real estate firms, contractors, and hundreds of small businesses across Dubai.

Our mission is simple: make professional printing as easy as sending a WhatsApp message. No portals, no accounts, no waiting for email quotes — just a real specialist who answers fast, checks your artwork for free, and delivers when promised.
